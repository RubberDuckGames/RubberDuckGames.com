How to play

Build a creature out of cells, then add it to the world.

Every cell uses a little energy all the time. A mouth eats the green food next to it. When a creature runs out of energy, it dies.

When a creature is full of energy, it has a baby and gives it half. With Babies change turned up, some babies are born with one cell added, taken away or swapped.

Change the sliders, or edit a copy of a creature, and watch what happens. Tools has Add food, Add poison, Add creature and Remove; pick one, then tap or drag on the world.

Add creature puts a copy of the creature in the builder wherever you tap.

Remove takes away creatures, food and poison wherever you tap or drag.

Save keeps a creature on this device, with its name. Its picture shows under the builder grid; tap it to build it again. To rename one, change its name and save it again. Each creature also gets a kind from its cells: Hunter, Plant, Grazer or Blob.

A producer cell makes energy, like a plant, so a creature with enough of them never needs to eat. Plants that grow close together join into one super plant, with a green box round the group; they share their energy.

An eye cell sees a few squares around it. A creature that can move heads for food it sees, and runs from any creature with a killer cell. A killer with an eye chases the creatures it sees.

A smart cell remembers where its creature last found food and goes back there, and remembers where it got hurt and keeps away for a while. Pick a smart creature to see its memories: a green ring for food, a red cross for danger.

A killer cell takes energy from any other creature it touches, unless it touches armor.

Poison blocks are solid. A creature touching one loses energy. Add poison drops them; with it on, tapping a poison block takes it away.

Zoom in with + and − (or pinch, or the mouse wheel) to see the cells up close, and drag to look around. Zoomed in, the view follows the creature you picked.

Disaster picks one at random: a meteor (armor saves creatures at its edge), an ice age (no food and no producing for a while, so storage helps), poison rain (armor blocks it, and the poison stays) or a flood (movers swim for dry land, fins swim and eat as if nothing happened; the rest need storage). Whether anything lives depends on how the creatures are built. With Surprise disasters ticked, one can also strike by itself now and then.

Keys: Space pauses, B builds, N picks the next creature, F turns Add food on and off, P turns Add poison on and off, C turns Add creature on and off, R turns Remove on and off, X starts a disaster, + and − zoom.
